SIEGE — an original top-down arena survival shooter.

Hold out against the waves. WASD to move; click to shoot toward the cursor and
hold the mouse for auto-fire (arrow keys also fire, eight directions). One life.

Written from scratch for chainrom, and deliberately a different engine from
DEPTH: DEPTH is a first-person raycaster, SIEGE is a 2-D top-down shooter. A
second game with nothing in common but the loader beneath it is the point — it
shows the on-chain reader boots whatever it verifies, not one game it was tuned
for. No asset files — the arena, the enemies and the font are all arithmetic —
so the payload is the engine and nothing else.

Source: engine-src/arena.c
