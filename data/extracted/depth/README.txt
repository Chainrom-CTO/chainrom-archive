DEPTH — an original first-person maze game.

Collect every lamp, then find the exit. Arrows or WASD to move, shift to run.

Written from scratch for chainrom: the raycasting renderer, the wall textures,
the level and the creatures are all defined in code. There are no asset files —
the textures are arithmetic and the map is a string literal — so the payload is
the engine and nothing else.

Source: engine-src/maze.c
